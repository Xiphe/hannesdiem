`ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19` of
[natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).
Input: `geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson`.

[Public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
No OpenStreetMap data, tiles, iframe, map SDK or runtime map requests.

`europe.svg` contains countries intersecting 26°W–46°E / 33–72°N, including neighboring land for geographic context. Rings are clipped to that window and simplified with Douglas–Peucker at .006°. Projection: x=(longitude−10.4)×65+500; y=(51.2−latitude)×100+500. Generated once from the already-downloaded Natural Earth source used by the parked invitation study; asset generation is not an application dependency.

The camera moves from Europe to the venue coordinates 53.9722597°N, 13.643702°E (projected 710.84063, 222.77403). The pointer shares the camera coordinate system. The finer .006° simplification retains coastline detail for the closer scroll zoom. This is illustrative geography, not a navigation map.
