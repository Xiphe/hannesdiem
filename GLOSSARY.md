# hannesdiem.de

## Language

**Release**: A published album, EP, or single with an ordered track list and listening links.

**Track**: A recording listed on a release; may reference a song or be instrumental/silent.

**Song**: A written work with lyrics and authors; may appear on multiple releases.

**Contributor**: A person or organization credited for work on a release.

**Credit**: A contributor's roles on a particular release, or an author's attribution for a song.
